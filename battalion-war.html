A game of prepared war

Battalion War

Two decks. Thirteen waves of four. You set the order — then the cards fight like War. Highest rank wins. Ties declare war.

Rules

  1. Each player receives a full 52-card deck and secretly orders it into 13 waves of 4.
  2. Play starts at Wave 1. When a wave is spent, the next wave continues the queue.
  3. Higher rank wins both cards into a capture pile (A high, 2 low, suits ignored).
  4. On a tie: each plays 3 face-down and 1 face-up. The face-up card takes the whole pot. Another tie stacks another war.
  5. If too few cards remain for a full war, play whatever is left; the last card is the decider. A last-card tie: each keeps their own card.
  6. When both queues are empty, the player with more captured cards wins.
